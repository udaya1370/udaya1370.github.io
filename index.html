Link to choose your own adventure: robbing the bank (perfect escape).

Context: For this project me and Gibran had to make a choose your own adventure story using the skills we’ve learned so far and mainly using communication and collaboration. Content: This is the funnest project that was assigned to the 10th grade this year. We had to work with the person who had similar idea's with what the story should be and my partner and I decided to make a choose your own adventure about robbing a bank with a fun secret ending and a normal ending but also challenges The choices you make decide what will happen and you have to choose the right answer some choices are easy to make but others can be difficult and without a reason to go a specific direction like go left or right which can either mean your doom and go to jail or to win with all the money and robbing the bank would be successful. Also even choosing how you may enter would you enter the front door or the back vent or maybe a window what would be your course of action that could take you to unfortunate and or fortunate endings. We accomplished creating this project by using Github command line pulling and pushing and of course editing while puting links and making plans for where they should go to and what should happen next. So what we did is split up the path in three directions and we made one really short but then gave eachother only one path to work off so we could make our idea's and be satisfied with the finished product after a couple days of work it was finished and we liked how the story grew into a fun adventure. Reflection: Some of the challenges my partner and I faced were focusing to make sure we had MVP. We struggled with this because we could get lost in the story and take to long to get the credit that is deserved and make sure that what we put down on the plan goes into the project itself. Our teacher, Mr. Muller helped us the best he could, by mentioning while we were working to not do too much to where it would be difficult to finish the project and story so we can get full credit on what was viable I was happy when i found a way to make the story nice and with many turning points and got it done on the project and the markdown files. My biggest takeaways from this project were properly communicating with a group member I wasn't too good at communicating hints but I was better at just giving answers. Before this project I struggled with adding links but thanks to Gibran who was my partner I was able to fully understand how to successfully add the link and push the new feature on to our project. Despite sitting next to each other Gibran and I never really spoke to one another about the project since we were naturally good at what we wanted to do and already split up the work so it was not that hard to do the project and get it done on my behalf. Throughout this project I learned to be patient and listen to his idea's and thought process behind the topic we wanted to do for the project. The next steps I would like to take is to learn how to motivate my partners to work and focus on what they need to do so we can chill later.
